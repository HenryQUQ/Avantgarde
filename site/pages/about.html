Our beginnings

A stronger voice.
A wider conversation.

Charlie Ji founded The Avant-garde Club alongside its founding members and a wider group of young, passionate AV professionals working across the UK and Europe.

Their aim was to bring the community together and give young professionals a stronger voice in the future of the industry.
