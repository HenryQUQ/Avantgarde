Our beginnings

A shared ambition

Charlie Ji founded the club alongside its founding members and a wider group of young, passionate AV professionals working across the UK and Europe.

Their aim was to bring the community together and give young professionals a stronger voice in the future of the industry.

Code of Conduct

Ethical conduct is a condition of membership.

The Code applies to everyone, from Club leadership to honorary members, at every webinar, roundtable, social event and introduction.

  1. 01

    Ethical conduct

    Act honestly, be accurate about qualifications and interests, and never pressure anyone for business, money or favours.

  2. 02

    Equality and respect

    Treat everyone fairly. No harassment, no discrimination, and no retaliation against anyone who raises a concern.

  3. 03

    Intellectual property

    Rights stay with their owner. Share only what you are authorised to, credit creators and agree ownership before joint work.

  4. 04

    Confidentiality

    Private discussions, roadmaps and member details stay private, and never go into public AI tools without permission.

  5. 05

    Conflicts of interest

    Declare relevant interests in writing, and step back from any decision they could affect.

  6. 06

    Independent decisions

    No price fixing, bid rigging or no-poach agreements, and no exchange of competitively sensitive information.

  7. 07

    Events and representation

    Respect venues, organisers and each other's choices, and use the Club's name and logo accurately.

  8. 08

    Fair resolution

    Concerns are reviewed by someone without a conflict, with the right to respond and to appeal.
